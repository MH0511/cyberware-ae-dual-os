# Cyberware AE — Cyberdeck & Sandevistan Dual OS Support

Unofficial addon for Cyberware-AE: use a cyberdeck and Sandevistan together, keep quickhacking available, and see separate HUD charge indicators.

**Controls:** your usual cyberware ability key uses Sandevistan outside the scanner and vanilla Overclock inside the scanner. Overclock must be unlocked. Charge, cooldown and gameplay restrictions remain in effect.

**Tested versions:** Cyberpunk 2077 2.5 / Cyberware-AE 1.01 / redscript 0.5.32 / Cyber Engine Tweaks 1.38.0, Windows PC. The tested combination was Apogee 5++ + NetWatch Netdriver 5++. Cyberware-AE 1.13 is not verified.

**Install:** close the game, back up saves, install prerequisites separately, then merge the archive's bin and r6 folders into the game folder. Buy an extra OS slot through AE and equip one deck and one Sandevistan. Restart the game; do not first hot-load the addon during gameplay.

**Uninstall:** with the addon active, unequip the extra Sandevistan at a ripperdoc, leave one cyberdeck installed, and create a new manual save. Exit and move r6/scripts/AEDualOS and bin/x64/plugins/cyber_engine_tweaks/mods/ae_dual_os out of the game directory. Keep AE itself if you want its slot expansion.

**Scope:** one deck plus one Sandevistan. Multiple decks, multiple Sandevistans, Berserk triple-OS and multiple arm combinations are unsupported. Do not combine with Cyberware-EX, System-EX or overlapping OS/HUD replacements. Save reload and HUD display were tested; all equipment changes, quest situations and extended combat were not exhaustively tested.

**Credits:** the equipment/focus/HUD design is adapted from Cyberware-EX by Pavel Siberx / psiberx, under MIT; the original license is included. Cyberware-AE by HilanderGK is a separate prerequisite. No AE or EX files are bundled. Neither upstream author has endorsed this addon.
